# What's new in XRP Studio

## Version 0.1.0

First version of XRP Studio, based on XRPWeb.

* Works without internet: install it as an app and it opens offline, with blocks, Python, firmware and drivers.
* My projects: your programs are saved on your device automatically, with a Trash and an Archive.
* Accounts and sync: sign in with Google to keep your projects in your account and share a tablet.
* Available in several languages, with a friendly and colourful look and light and dark themes.
* A new block editor with search and a Python editor with colours and suggestions.
* Updated libraries to keep everything fast and safe.

## Version 0.0.4

Plain Spanish blocks, the Trash and Archive, and a choice of editor.

### Blocks and language
* The Spanish blocks, help texts and categories were rewritten in plain words kids understand ("Avanzar 20 cm con fuerza 0.5").
* A Motors group in the toolbox, and on phones the toolbox is a column of icons.
### Projects
* Trash: deleting a project moves it to the Trash, where you can restore it or delete it for good (30 days).
* Archive: hide a project you do not need now without deleting it, and bring it back any time.
* Changes made on another device show up while you are signed in.
* Opening a project shows its tab right away with a loading message.
### Editor
* A Blocks | Python selector to choose how you see your program.
* Python code has more colours: function calls, attributes, constants and objects.
### Phones and tablets
* The bottom of the app stays on screen on Android phones, and the installed app opens in full screen.
### Under the hood
* Newer versions of React, Blockly, Tailwind, charts, the tab layout and the Python editor.

## Version 0.0.3

Phones and tablets, a faster app and automatic updates.

### Phones and tablets
* Phone width has a menu button with your account at the bottom.
* The blocks canvas zooms with two fingers, and phones get a small toolbox so more blocks fit on the screen.
### Updates
* The app updates itself when you open or reload it, with no notice to dismiss.
### Under the hood
* Faster builds, newer build tools, and automatic tests of accounts, sync and how the key screens look.

## Version 0.0.2

Installable app, automatic saving and projects that follow your account.

### Works anywhere
* Install XRP Studio as an app; after the first visit it opens without internet.
### Projects
* Your programs save themselves a moment after you stop typing, with a Saving / Saved indicator.
* Projects stay in the browser and, if you sign in with Google, sync with your account. Use "Keep me signed in" on your own device.
* Signing in keeps working for hours, and files that cannot be saved are reported.
### Layout
* An Explorer icon opens the folders; a Terminal icon shows or hides the Terminal.
* Panels are rounded cards, and the file tree has lighter rows and clearer Rename and Delete icons.

## Version 0.0.1

The first working version of the fork.

* Works without internet: an offline build with blocks, Python, firmware and drivers.
* My projects: programs live on your device, with no login needed.
* A friendlier top bar and welcome screen.
* Sign in with Google to keep projects in your account (login server included).
* The Run button copies your program to the robot.
