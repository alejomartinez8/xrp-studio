XRP Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

XRP Studio is a free, open-source web editor for programming the XRP robot with blocks and Python. It is a fork of XRPWeb. This page explains what data the app uses and why.

Without signing in

You can use XRP Studio without an account. Your projects and settings (language, theme, layout) are stored only in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). They never leave your device unless you copy a program to your robot.

Signing in with Google

Signing in with Google is optional. When you sign in, XRP Studio uses:

  • Your name, email address and profile picture, to show which account is signed in. They are only displayed in the app and are not stored on our server.
  • Google Drive, limited to files XRP Studio creates (the drive.file permission). The app saves your programs in an XRPCode folder in your Drive. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

To keep you signed in, the sign-in server stores Google's refresh token encrypted in a secure, HttpOnly cookie in your browser. The server has no database and keeps no copy of your data. Signing out revokes the token and deletes the cookie. You can also remove XRP Studio's access at any time from your Google account permissions.

XRP Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your Google data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Connecting a robot

When you connect an XRP robot, the app sends anonymous robot usage data to the XRP project's server at WPI (Worcester Polytechnic Institute): the last five characters of the robot's ID, the robot model and whether it was connected by Bluetooth. No personal information is included. This is inherited from XRPWeb and helps the XRP project understand how robots are used.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and operation.

Children

XRP Studio is designed for use in classrooms and at home. No account is needed to use it, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Signing in with Google is optional and follows the rules of the Google account used.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated. For questions, contact the maintainer through the project's GitHub repository.
